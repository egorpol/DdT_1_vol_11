# DdT 1, Vol. 11 — 0.13.1 (pre-release)

This is a patch release of the MEI edition of *Dietrich Buxtehudes Instrumentalwerke* (Denkmäler deutscher Tonkunst, first series, vol. 11, ed. Carl Stiehl, 1903). It settles the reading at Op. II/2, m. 268, the one annotation of 0.13.0 labelled `unresolved-reading`, as a source reading, and records that the entries of the Op. II print's own list of misprints that are filed under Sonata VII have been compared with the encoding. Neither changes the music. It has the same 17 works, and apart from these changes and the header metadata every MEI file is as in 0.13.0.

**Status: corrected, not finalized**, as for 0.12.0 to 0.13.0. The [0.12.0 release notes](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/release-notes/0.12.0.md) describe the contents, how to use the edition and its known limitations, and all of that holds for 0.13.1 with the changes since 0.12.0, which the [0.12.1](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/release-notes/0.12.1.md), [0.12.2](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/release-notes/0.12.2.md) and [0.13.0](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/release-notes/0.13.0.md) release notes describe. The annotation counts are given below, and the excerpts in `supplemental_material/` are those its [manifest](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/supplemental_material/manifest.json) lists, more than 0.12.0 had; none was added or removed since 0.13.0. The [1.0 checklist](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/finalization-plan.md) lists what remains before 1.0.

## Changes since 0.13.0

The [changelog](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/CHANGELOG.md) lists each change with its identifiers.

### Op. II/2, m. 268: a source reading

DdT prints the viola da gamba's bar as D3 eighth, eighth rest, quarter rest, half rest. The Op. II partbook (Viola da gamba, Sonata II, printed p. 10, eighth staff) printed D3 quarter, quarter rest, half rest; in the Uppsala copy a hand added a flag to the D3 and an eighth rest in ink. The print's list of misprints has an entry for the staff: "Fol. 10. In der achten Zeil/ muß die 21. Note 1/8 mit 1/8 Pause seyn." The 21st note is this D3, so the entry asks for an eighth note with an eighth rest, the reading that DdT prints and that the ink enters in the copy. In 0.13.0 the two fractions of the entry were not read with confidence, and the ink was taken for print, so the entry was recorded as an `unresolved-reading`. On 2026-11-20 enlarged crops of the bar, compared with the printed eighth note and eighth rest in m. 281 on the same page, and of the fractions, compared with others in the list, settled the reading: both fractions are 1/8. Egor Polyakov reviewed the crops and reclassified the annotation `editorial.op202.m268.errata` as `source-reading`; its text now gives this reading. Op. II/2's header and the excerpt's caption in the [manifest](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/supplemental_material/manifest.json) and [rights note](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/supplemental_material/README.md) say the same.

Where DdT already has the reading an entry of the list asks for, the [editorial policy](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/editorial-policy.md#sources-and-their-roles) adds nothing. It now names m. 268 as the one such entry that keeps an annotation, and gives the two reasons: its fractions are too small to read at page scale, and 0.13.0 recorded it as `unresolved-reading`.

The corpus has 108 editorial annotations, as in 0.13.0, all with a label: 56 conjectural emendations, 24 source-reading annotations, 15 source-supported emendations and 13 encoding notes. None is labelled `unresolved-reading`.

### The Sonata VII entries of the list of misprints

The six entries of the list that are filed under Sonata VII were compared with the encoding and with the partbook staves they name between 2026-11-19 and 2026-11-23. DdT has the corrected reading in each, so nothing is added. Op. II/7's header records the comparison; it had said that no other passage was compared with the print beyond the neighbouring bars of m. 167. The entries filed under Sonatas I, II, V, VI and VII have now been compared.

### What has not been compared

Settling m. 268 leaves no annotation labelled `unresolved-reading` in the corpus. That does not mean that every reading is settled, and it does not complete the comparison with the prints. The entries of the Op. II list filed under Sonatas III and IV have not yet been compared with the encoding, so the absence of a record says nothing about them. Eight further places, noted while the prints were read for other passages, have not yet been examined: three in the Op. I print, among them the tempo word at Op. I/4, m. 113, and five in the Op. II print, at Op. II/4, mm. 188 and 190, and in the continuo figures at Op. II/2, mm. 266 and 273, and Op. II/6, m. 113.

### Headers

All 17 headers now read `<edition n="0.13.1">`, the last-updated date is 2026-11-23, and each `revisionDesc` has an entry for this release.

## Identifiers

No `xml:id` of v0.13.0 is gone, none is new, and none names a different element. Measure IDs, numbers, zones and facsimile links match v0.13.0 in all 17 works. The [encoding profile](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/mei-encoding-profile.md#release-identity-versions-and-citation) treats a compatible correction or metadata fix that preserves event identity as a patch.

## Validation record

The consistency reports and their provenance, [`validation/editorial-reports.json`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/validation/editorial-reports.json), come from a full run completed 2026-11-23 (08:25 UTC) in review mode, with the software of 0.12.0 to 0.13.0: [CAMAT](https://pypi.org/project/camat/) 0.2.7 from PyPI, Verovio 6.3.0, lxml 6.1.2, saxonche 13.0.0, Python 3.14, the pinned official MEI 5.1 CMN schema and the edition profile `DdT_1_vol_11` 0.1.0.

- All 17 files passed XML and reference checks, RELAX NG, Schematron, resource and image-URL checks, full Verovio rendering and CAMAT event parsing. Their hashes were unchanged after the run.
- The edition's publication rules return **needs-review** for all 17, as expected for unfinalized files. The reports contain 286 rows (276 `warning`, 10 `info`): the rows of 0.13.0 apart from their line numbers, without the warning for the unresolved reading at Op. II/2, m. 268. The [0.12.0 release notes](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/release-notes/0.12.0.md#known-limitations) explain them under known limitations.
- The test suites pass (170 Python and 35 JavaScript tests). `scripts/regenerate_accid_ges.py --check` and `scripts/normalize_midi_data.py --check` find nothing to change. `scripts/musical_lint.py` lists 172 suspects, the same rows as for 0.13.0.
- Verovio 6.3.0's MIDI of v0.13.0 and of this release is byte for byte the same in all 17 works.

The release-mode gate was not applied: it accepts only finalized files with a recorded, hash-bound reviewer sign-off, which this pre-release does not claim.

To rerun the reports, the two data checks and the test suites of this release:

```sh
pip install -r requirements.txt
python scripts/refresh_editorial_reports.py --network
python scripts/regenerate_accid_ges.py --check
python scripts/normalize_midi_data.py --check
python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -q tests
node --test tests/js/*.test.mjs
```

`scripts/refresh_editorial_reports.py` validates each work in turn, as the recorded run did, and rewrites the tracked reports and `validation/editorial-reports.json` in place, so `git diff` shows any change; `--network` adds the image-URL checks. The pinned versions need Python 3.12 or newer, and the checks also need libxml2's `xmllint` on `PATH`. An empty or passing report means only that the automated checks found nothing; it does not establish that every pitch, rhythm or figure matches the source.

## Known limitations

Those of 0.12.0, listed in its [release notes](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/release-notes/0.12.0.md#known-limitations), as changed since: the excerpts in `supplemental_material/` are now those its manifest lists, not only the ones counted there. In particular, no work is finalized, each header's `pubStmt/identifier[@type='URI']` points to the file on `main`, not to this release, and transcription errors can remain: no accuracy figure is claimed for any part of the text. The entries of the Op. II list of misprints filed under Sonatas III and IV and the eight places named [above](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.1/docs/release-notes/0.13.1.md#what-has-not-been-compared) have not been examined.
