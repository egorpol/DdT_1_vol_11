# DdT 1, Vol. 11 — 0.13.0 (pre-release)

This is a minor release of the MEI edition of *Dietrich Buxtehudes Instrumentalwerke* (Denkmäler deutscher Tonkunst, first series, vol. 11, ed. Carl Stiehl, 1903). It compares the entries of the Op. II print's own list of misprints that are filed under Sonatas I, II, V and VI, adopts two of them in Op. II/2 and records five that it does not apply. It also adds a tie in Op. II/5, removes stray whitespace from 8 attribute values and corrects several header statements and one record. It has the same 17 works. Two `xml:id`s change by their whitespace, which makes it a minor step rather than a patch (see [Identifiers](#identifiers)).

**Status: corrected, not finalized**, as for 0.12.0 to 0.12.2. The [0.12.0 release notes](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/docs/release-notes/0.12.0.md) describe the contents, how to use the edition and its known limitations, and all of that holds for 0.13.0, with the annotation and excerpt counts given below. The [0.12.1](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/docs/release-notes/0.12.1.md) and [0.12.2](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/docs/release-notes/0.12.2.md) release notes describe the changes since 0.12.0. The [1.0 checklist](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/docs/finalization-plan.md) lists what remains before 1.0.

## Changes since 0.12.2

The [changelog](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/CHANGELOG.md) lists each change with its identifiers.

### The Op. II print's list of misprints

The [editorial policy](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/docs/editorial-policy.md#sources-and-their-roles) now treats the Op. II print's own list of misprints as part of the witness `src.uppsala.op2`. An entry that is legible and fits its bar attests the corrected reading; where DdT already has that reading, nothing is added. The policy gives the location and counting rules and says when an entry that is not applied is recorded in an annotation or in the work's `editorialDecl`. The entries filed under Sonatas I, II, V and VI were compared from 2026-11-15 to 2026-11-19. Those filed under III, IV and VII still await comparison, so the absence of a record says nothing about them.

Two entries change the music, both in Op. II/2:

- **m. 170, viola da gamba, beat 4:** F-sharp4-E4-F-sharp4 replaces DdT's A4-G4-A4. The partbook prints F-sharp4-G4-F-sharp4, and the print's list of misprints corrects it to F-sharp4-E4-F-sharp4; DdT's notes stand on the corrected staff positions, but under DdT's treble clef they sound a third higher. The source-supported-emendation annotation `editorial.op202.m170.errata` links two excerpts, the partbook's bar and the list's entry. The keyboard realization is unchanged.
- **m. 267, violin and keyboard realization:** the source-supported-emendation annotation `editorial.op202.m267.errata` cites the list's B-flat4 and links its entry. The violin's B4 at beat 3.25 takes a bracketed flat, and a second bracketed flat makes the B4 of DdT's realization at beat 3.5 B-flat4; the conjectural-emendation annotation `editorial.clash.op202-m267-b3_5` records that change. A sign in square brackets is the edition's, not DdT's.

Four entries are recorded in annotations, and the music is unchanged:

- **Op. II/1, m. 158, continuo:** the source-reading annotation `editorial.op201.m158.errata` records the entry for the E-flat3 minim and links an excerpt of the line with its stacked figures. DdT's figures are kept.
- **Op. II/2, m. 144, continuo:** the source-reading annotation `editorial.op202.m144.errata` links the entry asking for a flat before the A2 minim. DdT's A2 is kept as the resolution of the diminished seventh on G-sharp2.
- **Op. II/2, m. 268, viola da gamba:** the entry's note and rest values for the D3 cannot be read with confidence. DdT's reading, which is also the print's, is kept; the one reading that fills the bar and changes it gives D3 quarter, quarter rest, half rest. The annotation `editorial.op202.m268.errata` is the edition's first `unresolved-reading`: the validator reports it as a warning in review mode and as an error in release mode, so a release review must settle it.
- **Op. II/5, m. 31, violin:** the entry asks for two sixteenths, notes 15 and 16 on the partbook's staff, to be sixty-fourths. That would leave the 4/4 bar short by a dotted sixteenth, so the entry does not fit its bar; DdT's reading, which is also the print's, is kept (`editorial.op205.m31.errata`).

Op. II/1's header records one more entry, for the gamba on printed p. 4, fourth staff: it asks for a natural before the 25th note of a staff that holds 18 notes, so it is not applied.

The seven linked images are new excerpts in `supplemental_material/`: the partbook's bar at Op. II/2, m. 170 and six entries of the list. The [rights note](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/supplemental_material/README.md) covers them; each of its sections says why the image, not a transcription, is needed. The corpus now has 108 editorial annotations, all with a label: 56 conjectural emendations, 23 source-reading annotations, 15 source-supported emendations, 13 encoding notes and one unresolved reading.

### Op. II/5, m. 30: a tie

The tie that DdT prints from the violin's C-sharp6 quarter to the C-sharp6 sixteenth after it is added (`tie.op2_05.m30.cs6`); the encoding had no tie or slur there.

### Stray whitespace in attribute values

The OMR had left stray whitespace in 8 attribute values; each value now changes only by its whitespace. Three were in the assembled works, in every release since their work joined the corpus. In Op. I/1, the violin's E-flat5 in m. 63 (`njfovys1`) read `accid="f "` and now reads `accid="f"`: music21 10.5.0 rejected the file on it, while Verovio already sounded the flat that DdT prints. Two ids lose a leading space (see [Identifiers](#identifiers)). The page files carry the same three fixes and two more whose assembled works were already clean. The MEI schema accepts such values, so `tests/test_attribute_whitespace.py` now checks every tracked MEI file for them.

### Header statements and a record

- In Op. II/1, II/2, II/3, II/5, II/6 and II/7, the STIMU facsimile's availability statement describes the kinds of the work's excerpts and leaves their count to the [manifest](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/supplemental_material/manifest.json). In 0.12.0 to 0.12.2 it stated two excerpts for II/3 and one for II/6, where the manifest listed seven and two, and II/5 and II/7 had no excerpt statement. The five Op. I headers with an excerpt now describe it as of one printed bar or less. `tests/test_excerpt_headers.py` checks each statement against the manifest.
- The record of the first musical-lint triage pass (0.10.0) now says that, before approving the fixes, the editor compared each of the 351 corrections with the DdT 1903 page image. The records had said that the editor spot-checked eight of the model's findings, which understated the check. No list of the check was kept; the editor's statement of 2026-11-19 is its record. The 0.10.0 and 0.11.0 release notes keep their sentence as published, and a dated note at the end of each says so.

### Headers

All 17 headers now read `<edition n="0.13.0">`, the last-updated date is 2026-11-20, and each `revisionDesc` has an entry for this release.

## Identifiers

Two `xml:id`s of v0.12.2 change by their whitespace: `" wl394"` (Op. I/1, m. 38, a measure rest in the keyboard's left hand) is now `"wl394"`, and `" p8f9s6jn"` (Op. II/1, m. 8, a chord in the keyboard's right hand) is now `"p8f9s6jn"`. Each names the same element, and nothing in the corpus refers to either, but XML tools such as lxml keep the value as written, so a reference that stored the old value no longer finds its element. The [encoding profile](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/docs/mei-encoding-profile.md#release-identity-versions-and-citation) allows a patch only where event identity is preserved, so, as in 0.11.0 and 0.12.0, ids that are gone make this a minor step.

No other id of v0.12.2 is gone, and none names a different element. Ten are new: the seven annotations named above, the two editorial flats in Op. II/2, m. 267 (`accid.edit.xpxkhgu`, `accid.edit.knprbad`) and the tie in Op. II/5, m. 30. Measure IDs, numbers, zones and facsimile links match v0.12.2 in all 17 works.

## Validation record

The consistency reports and their provenance, [`validation/editorial-reports.json`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/validation/editorial-reports.json), come from a full run completed 2026-11-20 (01:04 UTC) in review mode, with the software of 0.12.0 to 0.12.2: [CAMAT](https://pypi.org/project/camat/) 0.2.7 from PyPI, Verovio 6.3.0, lxml 6.1.2, saxonche 13.0.0, Python 3.14, the pinned official MEI 5.1 CMN schema and the edition profile `DdT_1_vol_11` 0.1.0.

- All 17 files passed XML and reference checks, RELAX NG, Schematron, resource and image-URL checks, full Verovio rendering and CAMAT event parsing. Their hashes were unchanged after the run.
- The edition's publication rules return **needs-review** for all 17, as expected for unfinalized files. The reports contain 287 rows (277 `warning`, 10 `info`): the rows of 0.12.2 apart from their line numbers, and one new warning for the unresolved reading at Op. II/2, m. 268. The [0.12.0 release notes](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/docs/release-notes/0.12.0.md#known-limitations) explain the others under known limitations.
- The test suites pass (170 Python and 35 JavaScript tests). `scripts/regenerate_accid_ges.py --check` and `scripts/normalize_midi_data.py --check` find nothing to change. `scripts/musical_lint.py` lists 172 suspects: the 171 of 0.12.2 and an `event_id_removed` row for `" wl394"`, the id that lost its leading space. That check compares with v0.9.0, which did not yet contain Op. II/1 and so not `" p8f9s6jn"`.
- Verovio 6.3.0's MIDI of v0.12.2 and of this release is byte for byte the same in 15 of the 17 works, among them Op. I/1 and II/1. In Op. II/2 five notes change their MIDI pitch, the three of m. 170 and the two B4s of m. 267, which now sound B-flat4. In Op. II/5 the tie of m. 30 joins the two C-sharp6s into one note, and nothing else changes.

The release-mode gate was not applied: it accepts only finalized files with a recorded, hash-bound reviewer sign-off, which this pre-release does not claim, and it reports the unresolved reading at Op. II/2, m. 268 as an error.

To rerun the checks on this release:

```sh
pip install -r requirements.txt
python scripts/refresh_editorial_reports.py --network
python scripts/regenerate_accid_ges.py --check
python scripts/normalize_midi_data.py --check
python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -q tests
node --test tests/js/*.test.mjs
```

The first command validates each work in turn, as the recorded run did, and rewrites the tracked reports and `validation/editorial-reports.json` in place, so `git diff` shows any change; `--network` adds the image-URL checks. The pinned versions need Python 3.12 or newer, and the checks also need libxml2's `xmllint` on `PATH`. An empty or passing report means only that the automated checks found nothing; it does not establish that every pitch, rhythm or figure matches the source.

## Known limitations

Those of 0.12.0, listed in its [release notes](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.13.0/docs/release-notes/0.12.0.md#known-limitations). In particular, no work is finalized, each header's `pubStmt/identifier[@type='URI']` points to the file on `main`, not to this release, and transcription errors can remain: no accuracy figure is claimed for any part of the text. The reading at Op. II/2, m. 268 is unresolved, and the entries of the Op. II list of misprints filed under Sonatas III, IV and VII have not been compared.
