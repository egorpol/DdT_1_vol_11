# DdT 1, Vol. 11 — 0.10.0 (pre-release)

This is the second public release of the MEI edition of *Dietrich Buxtehudes Instrumentalwerke* (Denkmäler deutscher Tonkunst, first series, vol. 11, ed. Carl Stiehl, 1903). It is the first release that contains all 17 works as complete MEI 5.1 files, each measure linked to the Bayerische Staatsbibliothek facsimile. Op. II/1 is new; the other 16 carry the corrections made since v0.9.0.

**Status: corrected, not finalized.** This is a review snapshot of 2026-10-27. The musical text and the facsimile links of every work have been reviewed against DdT 1903, and their consistency reports are complete. Publication sign-off, final filenames, the release-mode gate and whether 1.0 covers 16 or 17 works are still open. The [1.0 checklist](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/docs/finalization-plan.md) lists what remains. The files can be used and cited now, but details may change before 1.0. Each change will be listed in the [changelog](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/CHANGELOG.md).

## Changes since 0.9.0

### Sounding pitch and MIDI

In 0.9.0, Verovio's MIDI output and music21's MEI import, which take pitch only from each note's own accidental values, played thousands of notes natural that the notation makes sharp or flat (key signature, accidentals carried to the barline, ties). In 0.10.0 every note's `@accid.ges` is regenerated from CAMAT's resolution of the written notation ([`scripts/regenerate_accid_ges.py`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/scripts/regenerate_accid_ges.py); [`tests/test_accid_ges.py`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/tests/test_accid_ges.py) keeps it in step; the [encoding profile](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/docs/mei-encoding-profile.md#sounding-pitch-and-midi) states the rule). The written notation the regeneration reads is unchanged by it, and no event id changes with it.

Comparing per-note values the way Verovio MIDI reads them, for the 16 works of 0.9.0 and each note whose pitch name and octave are unchanged: 4,771 notes change from natural to sharp or flat (4,753 through the regeneration, 18 through printed accidentals the lint corrections added); 272 change from sharp or flat to natural (253 unfounded `accid.ges` and 8 unprinted written accidentals removed by the lint corrections, 11 by the regeneration: Op. I/5 mm. 144–145, Op. II/2 mm. 171, 185 and 189, Op. II/5 m. 162 and App. I m. 82); 1 note changes from flat to sharp (Op. II/6, `m1bebl2x`). Separately, 18 notes have a corrected pitch name or octave and 4 notes are new. In Op. II/1, new in this release, the regeneration gave 1,894 of its notes the sharp or flat that a per-note reader would otherwise have missed.

The MIDI instruments and tempi are still unedited; see Known limitations.

## Using the edition

### Choosing staves for analysis

The files keep DdT's printed score, including the two-staff keyboard realization of the figured bass that the 1903 edition added. This realization is DdT's, not Buxtehude's. State which view an analysis uses:

- **Solo parts and continuo:** staves 1–3 (violin, viola da gamba, figured bass), or staves 1–4 in App. II (two violins, gamba, figured bass). In App. III, staves 1–3 are gamba, violone and figured bass.
- **DdT's complete texture:** all staves, including the two realization staves (4–5, or 5–6 in App. II).

Per-work staff roles are listed under `staff_roles` in [`validation/editorial-works.json`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/validation/editorial-works.json), keyed by staff number, with the labels `violin`, `violin_1`, `violin_2`, `viola_da_gamba`, `violone`, `figured_continuo_bass`, and `keyboard_realization_upper`/`keyboard_realization_lower` for DdT's realization. A selection of the solo and continuo staves still comes from this corrected DdT text: it is not a transcription of the original partbooks. See the [editorial policy](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/docs/editorial-policy.md#staff-layout-and-analytical-views).

### Sounding pitch

Every note without a printed accidental that sounds sharp or flat carries `@accid.ges` (on its `<accid>` child, if it has one), derived from CAMAT's resolution of the written notation: the key signature, printed accidentals held for their staff, letter and octave to the barline, and ties. A note with a printed accidental takes its pitch from that accidental and carries no `@accid.ges`. Verovio MIDI and music21's MEI import read pitch only from each note's own values, so a reader of sounding pitch should take `@accid.ges` where present and the written accidental otherwise. The [encoding profile](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/docs/mei-encoding-profile.md#sounding-pitch-and-midi) states the rule.

### Finding the facsimile

Every `measure/@facs` points to a measure zone on a `surface`. Each surface stands for one DdT page: its `@corresp` is the BSB IIIF canvas and its `graphic/@target` the IIIF image. Printed DdT page *p* is BSB image *p* + 10, so page 3 is image `00013`. The images stay on the BSB server; this repository does not copy them. The full digitized volume is at [digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb00023199](https://digitale-sammlungen.de/en/view/bsb00023199) (URN `urn:nbn:de:bvb:12-bsb00023199-0`).

A web viewer is live at https://egorpol.github.io/DdT_1_vol_11/. It is rebuilt from `main`, not from the tag, so it may show later changes than this release. To see score and facsimile side by side locally, open `mei_facsimile_viewer.ipynb` (requires CAMAT, see below), or load a file into [mei-friend](https://mei-friend.mdw.ac.at/).

### Editorial annotations

Annotations sit inside the measure they concern, as `annot[@type='editorial']`. `@label` gives the category, `@plist` the affected events, `@resp` the responsible editor and `@source` the witness that attests the reading, if any. The categories are defined in the [editorial policy](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/docs/editorial-policy.md#correction-categories).

## Validation record

The consistency reports and their provenance, [`validation/editorial-reports.json`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/validation/editorial-reports.json), come from a full run completed 2026-10-27 (04:57 UTC) in review mode:

- Software: [CAMAT](https://pypi.org/project/camat/) 0.2.7 from PyPI, Verovio 6.3.0, lxml 6.1.2, saxonche 13.0.0, Python 3.14. Official MEI 5.1 CMN schema, pinned in [`schemas/`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/tree/v0.10.0/schemas) (SHA-256 `f6440d5e…`). Edition profile `DdT_1_vol_11` 0.1.0 ([`validation/profile.json`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/validation/profile.json)).
- All 17 files passed XML and reference checks, RELAX NG, Schematron, resource and image-URL checks, full Verovio rendering and CAMAT event parsing. Their hashes were unchanged after the run.
- The edition's publication rules return **needs-review** for all 17, as expected for unfinalized files. The reports contain 286 rows (276 `warning`, 10 `info`), all explained below under known limitations: 181 Schematron advisories on the IIIF canvas links (one per surface) plus 17 matching edition reminders, 78 short-voice warnings in the passages kept as `metcon="false"`, and 10 info rows on spelling or punctuation variants of tempo and other directions, such as `(Allegro.)` beside `Allegro`.
- The provenance file records each input and report hash, the CAMAT version and source hash, dependency versions and the checks that ran.

The release-mode gate was not applied: it accepts only finalized files with a recorded, hash-bound reviewer sign-off, which this pre-release does not claim.

To rerun the checks on this release:

```sh
pip install -r requirements.txt
python scripts/validate_corpus.py --manifest validation/editorial-works.json --mode review --verovio --camat-parse
python -m pytest -p no:cacheprovider -q tests
```

The pinned versions need Python 3.12 or newer, and the checks also need libxml2's `xmllint` on `PATH`. The [tool guide](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/validation/README.md) explains each check and report column. An empty or passing report means only that the automated checks found nothing; it does not establish that every pitch, rhythm or figure matches the source.

## Known limitations

- **Not finalized.** No work has a reviewer sign-off, filenames keep `_corr`, and each header's `pubStmt/identifier[@type='URI']` points to the file on `main`, not to this release. Cite the release tag (below) for a fixed version. Whether 1.0 covers 16 or 17 works is still open.
- **Selective witness comparison.** The edition is based on DdT 1903. The Uppsala prints and manuscript are cited for selected passages in Op. I/1, Op. II/1, Op. II/2, Op. II/6 and App. II; no complete collation is claimed.
- **App. II, m. 156, violin II** is an interim conjectural emendation (annotation `ayvjvlf1` in the App. II file): one eighth rest is omitted to restore the 3/4 meter. The reading of the Uppsala manuscript has not been confirmed.
- **Measures marked `metcon="false"`** (28) are intentional: the 12 pickup and complementary ending measures of App. I, and Op. II/2 mm. 199–214, where a 12/8 part sounds against common-time accompaniment. They account for the 78 short-voice warnings in the reports; the MEI headers and annotations explain them.
- **IIIF canvas warnings.** The official Schematron reads `surface/@corresp` as a local ID and flags each external canvas URL, once per surface (181). How to treat them is a decision for 1.0; no waiver is configured.
- **Rendering in Verovio 6.3.0** (details in [`docs/issues.md`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/blob/v0.10.0/docs/issues.md)):
  - Op. I/3, m. 17: one printed C4 notehead shared by two voices is drawn as two heads;
  - Op. I/7, m. 250: the parentheses around the 6–4–2 figure stack are drawn beside the top and bottom figures, not around the stack.
- **MIDI instruments and tempo are not yet edited.** All 129 `instrDef` elements are `midi.instrnum="0"` (piano), and violin and keyboard realization share MIDI channel 0. 34 `<tempo>` elements in 5 works (App. II 15, Op. II/3 13, Op. I/3, I/4 and I/6 2 each) carry OMR `midi.bpm` values from 52.5 to 190, and 27 carry `mm="120"`, 21 of which disagree with their own `midi.bpm`. These values come from the OMR, not from the print. They are scheduled for removal in a later release, with tempo left to the player.
- **Passages still awaiting a page check or re-transcription** (found by the lint, not yet corrected): Op. I/5 mm. 112–168, figured-bass timing (118 of 138 figures still placed in quarter-note units); Op. II/3 m. 112, staff 3 has a spacer (`gf8l7ey`) where DdT prints notes; Op. II/3 m. 231, the violin is incomplete; Op. I/6 m. 14, staff 4 lacks the tie into `i1u3sd4g`; page checks of Op. II/3 m. 43 (written F-flat3, `q1i2egi7`, `qhx89nu`), m. 227 (`hvgtzs4`) and m. 230 (written C-flat2, `itqadf7`), App. I m. 82 (violin B-flat4 `c1ftgyd6` against keyboard B4 `hitivn4`), and the gamba part of Op. I/2 m. 36.
- **Images.** The STIMU 1991 scans of the Uppsala prints are not published; only the four one-measure excerpts in [`supplemental_material/`](https://github.com/egorpol/DdT_1_vol_11/tree/v0.10.0/supplemental_material) are included, under the quotation basis described there.
